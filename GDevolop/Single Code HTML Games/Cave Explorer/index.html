🎮 MISSION BRIEFING

🚀 The Mission

Navigate through dangerous cave systems filled with strategically placed bats, rock hazards, and power-ups. Master your jetpack and fuel management!

⚡ Game Elements

  • 🦇 Cave Bats: Fixed positions that move toward you - time your movements carefully
  • 🪨 Rock Hazards: Strategically placed obstacles in challenging positions
  • 🔋 Battery Packs: Fully restore your jetpack fuel - collect regularly
  • 🛡️ Immunity Shields: 5 seconds of invincibility for dangerous sections

🎮 Controls

PC: WASD or Arrows = Jetpack • SPACE = Emergency Dash

Mobile: Virtual Joystick + Dash Button

🏆 Pro Strategies

  • Bats spawn at fixed positions but move toward you - plan your route
  • Collect battery packs before your fuel gets critically low
  • Use immunity shields before entering high-risk areas
  • Emergency dash through tight spots to escape danger
